# Learn by Doing

Learning sticks better when I turn ideas into small, practical exercises. I do not just read about a topic; I try it in a controlled way, note what happened, and then refine my understanding.

## My process

1. Start with one clear question.
2. Read a few reliable sources.
3. Test the idea in a safe environment.
4. Record the result in plain language.
5. Review and improve the note later.

## Why this matters

A concept can look simple in theory but still feel fuzzy in practice. When I write down the steps, mistakes, and lessons, it becomes much easier to remember and apply later.

## What I track

- the question I was trying to answer
- the main idea or concept
- what I changed or tested
- what worked, what failed, and why
- the next thing to revisit

Good notes are not about perfection. They are about making progress visible and keeping useful knowledge easy to recover.
