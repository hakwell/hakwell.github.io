# Ethics in Security

Cybersecurity is powerful, and with that power comes responsibility. I try to keep curiosity grounded in permission, care, and honesty.

## Permission first

I only work on systems I own or have explicit authorization to test. If a scope is unclear, I stop and ask before doing anything invasive.

## Minimal impact

I prefer the least intrusive method that answers the question. I avoid unnecessary access, unnecessary changes, and any action that could disrupt normal service.

## Respect for data

Sensitive information should be handled carefully. I avoid exposing personal details, credentials, or other private material in notes, reports, or public discussion.

## Honest conclusions

I separate what I observed from what I inferred. If something is uncertain, I say so. Good security work depends on accurate reporting, not speculation.

Ethics is not a side topic. It is part of doing technical work responsibly.
