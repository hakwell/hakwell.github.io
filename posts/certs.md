# Certification Notes

Certifications help me structure my learning, but the real value is in the understanding behind the exam objectives.

## Useful study habits

- break large topics into smaller questions
- review official objectives before deep study
- explain ideas in my own words
- use practice questions to find weak spots
- revisit difficult topics with fresh context

## Notes that help

I keep short notes with:

- the main concept
- key terms
- reliable references
- examples or practice scenarios
- questions I still need to answer

## Maintain momentum

A realistic study plan is easier to sustain than an intense one that burns out quickly. I focus on consistency, review, and progress over time.

A certification is a milestone, not the end of learning.
